Metric screw size chart

How to read a metric size

M6 means a 6 mm nominal diameter. M6 x 1 adds the pitch, the distance between threads, in millimetres. When no pitch is written the coarse pitch is assumed: 1 mm for M6, 1.25 mm for M8. Fine pitches exist for most sizes and are always written out, for example M8 x 1.

Tap drill and clearance hole

For a standard 60 degree thread the tap drill is the diameter minus the pitch, so M6 takes a 5 mm drill. Clearance holes follow ISO 273 in three classes. Fine suits accurate positioning, medium is the usual choice, and coarse tolerates more misalignment between parts. For M6 the three are 6.4, 6.6 and 7 mm.

Metric versus inch

The two systems are close but not interchangeable. M6 is 6 mm across and 1/4 inch is 6.35 mm, and the pitches differ, so a metric screw in an inch thread will bind or strip. Check the label before you drill.
